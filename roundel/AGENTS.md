# Roundel maintenance instructions

These instructions apply to every file in `roundel/`. Keep the sign generator
working as a standalone static page and preserve user-authored changes that are
unrelated to the task at hand.

## Wall Style custom shadow

Read this section before changing the `wallMount` preset, the `stone-wall`
scene, `syncWallMountGeometry()`, the wall shadow path helpers, or the
`#wall-mount-shadow` SVG layer.

The Wall Style shadow is deliberately custom SVG geometry. It is not a CSS/SVG
`drop-shadow`, a blurred duplicate of the sign, or a stack of simulated depth
layers. Its shape is swept from the visible sign silhouette toward the lower
right.

### Required invariants

- The ring and bar casts live in the same `#wall-depth-cast` group.
- Apply opacity once to that parent group. Do not give the ring and bar separate
  partial opacity: their overlap would become visibly darker.
- Both casts use the same `#wall-cast-gradient`, with
  `gradientUnits="userSpaceOnUse"`, so an overlap cannot reveal two differently
  scaled gradients.
- The bar shadow starts at the outermost contour of the opaque gray outline.
  `syncWallMountGeometry()` therefore expands the bar fill bounds by half the
  outline stroke on every side and increases the corner radius by the same
  amount.
- The bar shadow is one rounded-rectangle sweep. Its upper-right and lower-left
  edges leave the rounded outline at tangent points calculated by
  `setRoundedRectSweepPath()`. They must remain single continuous diagonal
  lines with no fork, wedge, step, or light sliver.
- The bar cast starts directly at the outlined face and uses the full
  `wallShadowOffset`. Do not restore a separate near-depth/body offset.
- The ring cast uses the outer edge of the outer outline and the inner edge of
  the inner outline. It remains one even-odd path generated by
  `setSweptRingShadowPath()`.
- The near part of the cast is darker and the far part is lighter, but there is
  only one continuous gradient per shared shadow layer.
- Nothing from the shadow may protrude above or to the left of the sign's outer
  gray contour.

### Regressions not to reintroduce

Previous iterations exposed these failure modes:

- Separate translucent ring and bar paths produced dark bands where the two
  shadows crossed.
- A separate 12 px bar back/body layer produced two shadows and split the
  upper-right and lower-left diagonals.
- Rectangular connector polygons ignored the rounded outline and created square
  fins at the corners.
- A duplicate `clipPath` around the bar produced extra antialiased edges and
  corner slivers.
- Sharp black rims or several concentric ring-shadow layers produced an
  incorrect gray/dark/gray stack and a light lower halo.
- Broad blur did not solve incorrect geometry; it only softened the wrong
  corner while leaving its silhouette visible.

### Visual verification

After changing Wall Style, inspect all of the following at high zoom:

1. Upper-right and lower-left bar corners: one diagonal must begin exactly at
   the rounded gray outline.
2. Ring/bar shadow crossings: the color and opacity must remain uniform, with
   no darker intersection band.
3. Ring outer and inner edges: gray outline followed by one dark-to-light cast,
   without extra concentric contours.
4. Top and left edges of both sign parts: no shadow pixel may leak outside the
   outline.
5. The gray bar outline must remain opaque; the red ring must not show through
   it.

The page is static and uses cache-busted assets. After changing runtime behavior
in `app.js`, update its `?v=` value in `index.html`; do the same for `styles.css`
when its rendered output changes. At minimum, run:

```sh
node --check roundel/app.js
perl -0777 -ne 'if (/<svg\b[^>]*id="roundel-svg"[\s\S]*?<\/svg>/) { print $& }' roundel/index.html | xmllint --noout -
git diff --check
```
