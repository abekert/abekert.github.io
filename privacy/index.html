Privacy

Small-site analytics, without analytics cookies

This website uses Google Analytics 4 only to produce aggregate statistics that help improve its pages and tools. Advertising, remarketing, Google Signals, advertising personalisation, and granular location and device collection are disabled.

Analytics control

Your choice is stored only in this browser as a functional preference. This privacy page itself does not load Google Analytics.

What is collected

Google Analytics receives cookieless measurements such as page views and a small set of coarse interaction events. Page URLs and referrers are stripped of query parameters and fragments before collection. For the Roundel Sign Maker, analytics events may describe actions such as opening the share menu or downloading an image, but never include sign text, project state, filenames, file contents, or share URLs.

Google may process network and basic browser information when a measurement request is made. Google states that GA4 discards raw IP addresses before they are logged or stored. No analytics client ID is stored while Analytics consent remains denied.

Storage and sharing

Analytics storage, advertising storage, advertising user data, and advertising personalisation are permanently set to denied. The GA4 property is not linked to Google Ads, data sharing with Google products is disabled, and event-level retention is set to two months. Standard aggregate reports may remain available for longer.

The Roundel Sign Maker separately uses local storage to preserve the artwork and first-run state on your own device. That functional state is not sent to Analytics.

Third-party content

Archived project pages can include Google Fonts and YouTube videos. YouTube embeds use privacy-enhanced mode, but loading or playing third-party content still creates a direct connection to that provider and is governed by its terms.

Contact

For privacy questions, email bekert@me.com. See also Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data safeguards.
